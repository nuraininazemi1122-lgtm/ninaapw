Mission: Impossible – The Final Reckoning (2025) follows Ethan Hunt as he races to stop a rogue, global artificial intelligence called "The Entity," which has divided people and is seizing control of nuclear systems. Hunt, aided by the IMF and new allies, must retrieve the key to the AI's source code, located on a sunken Russian submarine, before it plunges the world into global conflict. The film is the eighth installment in the series and a direct continuation of Dead Reckoning Part One

Mission: Impossible follows Ethan Hunt, a top agent of the Impossible Mission Force (IMF), a secret government agency tasked with carrying out high-risk covert operations. Across the films, Ethan and his team face impossible challenges, from preventing global catastrophes to stopping rogue agents and criminal organizations. Blending high-tech espionage, daring stunts, and complex twists, the series showcases Ethan’s determination, loyalty, and resourcefulness in completing missions against all odds.

In the first Mission: Impossible (1996), Ethan Hunt is framed for betraying his IMF team during a mission gone wrong in Prague. Branded a traitor and hunted by his own agency, Ethan must uncover the real mole who orchestrated the setup. With the help of a small group of allies, he takes on a dangerous mission to steal a list of undercover agents from the heavily guarded CIA headquarters. Packed with suspense, betrayal, and iconic action sequences, the film sets the tone for the thrilling franchise that follows.
